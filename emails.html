Implementation Guidelines

Retry Schedule

  • Immediate retry after initial failure
  • Second retry 12 hours later
  • Third retry 24 hours later
  • Final retry 36 hours later
  • Cancel booking at 48 hours if all retries fail

Payment Recovery Best Practices

  • Use one-click payment update links with pre-filled customer data
  • Support multiple payment methods (card update, PayPal, bank transfer)
  • Log all retry attempts and email opens for follow-up
  • Provide phone support option in every email
  • Never shame or blame the customer

Legal & Compliance

  • Clearly state cancellation timeline and consequences
  • Include opt-out for transactional emails (where legally required)
  • Store payment retry history for dispute resolution
  • Comply with PCI-DSS: never include full card numbers in emails
